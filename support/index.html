Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. MyLater does not require an account.

Where are my saved items stored?

On your device. MyLater stores your library locally.

How do I save something from another app?

Open the iOS Share Sheet and choose MyLater.

Why does an item first show a plain title, then change?

MyLater saves instantly, then fetches the real title and image in the background. The card updates itself a moment later.

How do I turn reminders off?

From MyLater's Profile screen, or by disabling MyLater notifications in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Remove individual items inside the app. Since there is no server-side account, deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to Apple's backup mechanisms.
