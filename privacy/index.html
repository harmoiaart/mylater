MyLater Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

MyLater is designed to help you save things you want to revisit later while minimising the amount of personal information collected. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the MyLater iOS application.

1. Information you save

MyLater lets you save links, text, images, titles, descriptions, categories and other information related to items you want to revisit. In the current version, your saved library is stored locally on your device. MyLater does not require you to create an account.

2. Information we collect

The current version of MyLater does not collect personal information on our servers. We do not require your name, email address, phone number or other account information to use the app. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Links and metadata

When you save a web link, MyLater may request that webpage or a publicly available service in order to retrieve information such as a page title, description or image needed to display the saved item. Requests to external websites may reveal standard network information, such as your IP address, to those services. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Share Extension

MyLater includes an iOS Share Extension that lets you save supported content from other applications. Information you choose to share with MyLater is used to create the saved item in your library.

5. Photos

If you add content using a photo, MyLater requests access only to the photos you explicitly select. The selected content is used solely to provide the feature you requested.

6. Notifications

MyLater may request permission to send notifications. Notifications are optional and can be disabled at any time from MyLater's Profile screen or in iOS Settings. The current version uses local notifications only.

7. Data storage

MyLater is a local-first application. Saved items and preferences are stored on your device. Because there is no user account or cloud synchronisation, deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored MyLater data, subject to Apple's device backup mechanisms.

8. Analytics and advertising

The current version of MyLater contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs, and does not use your saved content for targeted advertising. If such services are introduced in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your saved content or personal information. Information is communicated to external websites only when necessary to perform a function you requested, such as retrieving metadata for a link you saved.

10. Data retention and deletion

You can remove individual saved items from within the app. Locally stored application data can also be removed by deleting the application from your device, subject to Apple's backup services. There is no server-side account requiring a separate deletion request.

11. Children's privacy

MyLater is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when MyLater's features or data practices change. Material changes will be published with a revised effective date.
